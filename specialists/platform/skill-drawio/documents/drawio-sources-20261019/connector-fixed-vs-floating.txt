Connectors in draw.io attach to shapes in two different ways:

**Floating connectors** move around the perimeter of a shape as you move it to another location on the drawing canvas, or when you drag a connector and add way points to change the route it takes.

**Fixed connectors** stay attached to fixed points on your shapes, even when you move the shapes around the drawing canvas.

![Floating connectors move intelligently around the shape when you move it. Fixed connectors stay attached to that fixed point on the shape.](/img/blog/connector-floating-vs-fixed.gif)

You can have a fixed connection at one end of your connector and a floating connection at the other, or use the same type of connection at both ends.

## Draw a floating connector[​](#draw-a-floating-connector "Direct link to Draw a floating connector")

1. Hover over an existing shape, click and drag a connector from one of the four directional arrows.
2. Hover over the target shape and drop the connector end when the shape outline is blue.
     
   ![Floating connector - Drop the connector on the target shape when the outline is blue](/img/blog/connector-floating.png)
     
   The end of the connector will be blue with a circle to indicate it's a floating connection.

## Draw a fixed connector[​](#draw-a-fixed-connector "Direct link to Draw a fixed connector")

1. Hover over an existing shape on the drawing canvas to see the fixed connection points (small crosses, highlighted in green when you hover over them). Click and drag a connector from one of these points.
2. Hover over the target shape. Drop the connector end over a fixed connection point on the target shape when it is highlighted in green.
     
   ![Fixed connector - Drop the connector on a connection point - a small x - when it is highlighted with a green circle](/img/blog/connector-fixed.png)
     
   The end of the connector will be green with a cross to indicate it's a fixed connection.

**Tip:** Alternatively, hover over anywhere on the outline of a shape until it is highlighted in green. When you drop the connector to attach it, it will be fixed at that position on the shape's outline.

Fixed connection points are usually spaced evenly around the outside of a shape. You can [customise the shape connection points](/docs/manual/shapes/shape-connection-points-customise/) on any shape.

In addition to the fixed connection points around a shape, you can drop a fixed connection on the outline of a shape when it is highlighted green, and drop a [fixed connection anywhere within a shape](/docs/manual/connectors/connect-shapes/): Hold down `Shift` as you drag the connector into position over the target shape, then drop the connector when you see the green shape outline.
  
![Hold down Alt key as you connect to a shape to connect to any position on that shape](/img/blog/connect-to-shapes-anywhere.gif)

**Note:** If you [don't see any connection points](/docs/manual/shapes/shape-connection-points-customise/) when you hover over a shape, make sure you have the option enabled. Check the *Arrange* tab of the format panel, or select *View > Connection Points* from the menu.

The [*Snap to Point* shape property](/docs/manual/shapes/snap-to-point/) will make floating connectors, snap to the nearest connection point.
