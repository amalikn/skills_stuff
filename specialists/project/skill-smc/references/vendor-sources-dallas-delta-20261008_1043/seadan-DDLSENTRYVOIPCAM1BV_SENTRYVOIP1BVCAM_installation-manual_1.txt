4.1 Wiring - Power Over Ethernet (PoE)


  The DDC_VoIP telephone may be powered from the 2 way connector marked ‘Power’ on the side of the unit,
  see page 26. The polarity to this connector must be correct for the unit to function. (Note that NO damage
  will occur if it is connected incorrectly).
  The voltage input to this connection may be from 9 to 48 Volts.

  The alternative way to power the DDC_VoIP telephone is via the Ethernet connector using a PoE switch.
  The DDC_VoIP telephone is adapted to use a class 0 form of power source.

  There are two methods that power can be supplied from a Ethernet Switch,
  Mode A, supply power over the Tx/Rx pair, (TX pair pins 1&2 and RX pair pins 3&6).
  Mode B, via the spare pairs 4&5 and 7&8.
