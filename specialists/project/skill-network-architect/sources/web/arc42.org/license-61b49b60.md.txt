## What this means for companies

You can copy, modify and use arc42 inside your organization without ever publishing anything.

**Copy and modify freely.** Restructure the template, drop chapters, add your own, rename it, rebuild it in Confluence, Word or any other tool you like.

**Internal adaptation is not sharing.** The ShareAlike condition applies when you make an adapted version of the *template* available outside your organization. Whatever you change for your own teams stays yours, there is no obligation to publish it.

**Attribution is required whenever you pass the template or an adaptation on to others.** Credit Gernot Starke and Peter Hruschka, link to the license and say that you changed something. For purely internal use we simply appreciate the credit.

**If you do publish an adapted template**, on your website, in a public repository or as part of a product you ship, then that adapted template carries CC BY-SA 4.0 as well.

## Your architecture content remains yours

The license applies to the arc42 template itself, meaning its structure, its headings and its explanatory text. Content that you create and place inside the template remains your own. You may use, protect or publish that content according to your own rules. Publishing an architecture document in arc42 structure does not place your content under CC BY-SA.
