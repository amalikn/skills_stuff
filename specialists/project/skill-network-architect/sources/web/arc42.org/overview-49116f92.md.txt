## arc42 at a glance

arc42 answers two questions in a pragmatic way: **what** should you document and communicate about your architecture, and **how**. Twelve sections, each with a clear purpose, tailorable to your specific needs.

Open source and free, also for commercial use. Proven in practice since 2005, used by teams worldwide.

Every tile links straight to its detailed documentation on [docs.arc42.org](https://docs.arc42.org/): purpose, contents, motivation, and practical tips for each section.
