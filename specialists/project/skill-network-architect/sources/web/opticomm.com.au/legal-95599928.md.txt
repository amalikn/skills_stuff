## Functional Separation Undertaking (ACCC)

Opticomm is part of Uniti Group Limited (Uniti) who entered into a [Functional Separation Undertaking](https://www.accc.gov.au/public-registers/telecommunications-registers/functional-separation-undertakings-register/uniti-joint-functional-separation-undertaking) with the Australian Competition and Consumer Commission (ACCC). Any complaint regarding compliance with the Functional Separation Undertaking can be submitted directly to Uniti via email to [FSUcompliance@unitigrouplimited.com](mailto:FSUcompliance@unitigrouplimited.com "mailto:FSUcompliance@unitigrouplimited.com") or to the ACCC via [www.accc.gov.au](https://www.accc.gov.au/).
