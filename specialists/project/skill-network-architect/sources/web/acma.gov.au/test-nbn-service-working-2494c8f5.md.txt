              <h2 class="content-anchor" id="transferring-your-nbn-service-to-another-provider">
          Transferring your NBN service to another provider
                  </h2>
      
      
            <div class="field field--name-field-html field--type-text-long field--label-hidden field__item"><p>To move your current NBN service at your address to a different telco, you will need to give the new telco your ‘AVC ID’. </p>
<p>The AVC ID is a unique identifier with ‘AVC’ followed by 12 numbers, such as: AVC123456789100. Only you and your current telco have access to your AVC ID, so your service can’t be moved without your authorisation.</p>
<p>You’ll need to know your existing AVC ID before you ask another telco for a transfer.</p>
</div>
      

        </div>
  </div>

</div>
                  <div class="field__item">
  <div class="apply-tooltip paragraph body-copy paragraph--type--html paragraph--view-mode--default">
    <div class="paragraph-content">
    
              <h2 class="content-anchor" id="where-to-find-your-avc-id">
          Where to find your AVC ID
                  </h2>
      
      
            <div class="field field--name-field-html field--type-text-long field--label-hidden field__item"><p>You can ask your telco for your AVC ID, or it will be on your bill, your telco’s web portal or app.</p>
<p>Once you change providers, your new telco will give you a new AVC ID. </p>
<p>Find out more about <a href="https://www.austelco.org.au/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/G662_2021.pdf">NBN transfers</a>.</p>
