## Debian Long Term Support

Debian Long Term Support (LTS) is a project to extend the lifetime of all Debian stable releases to (at least) 5 years. Debian LTS is handled by a group of volunteers and companies interested in making it a success.

Debian LTS is not handled by the Debian Security and Release teams. Thus the Debian LTS team takes over security maintenance of the various releases once the Debian Security team stops its work.

![/!\](/htdocs/debwiki/img/alert.png "/!\") For more information see [LTS/Bookworm](/LTS/Bookworm), [LTS/Bullseye](/LTS/Bullseye), [LTS/Using](/LTS/Using), [LTS/Installing](/LTS/Installing) and [LTS/FAQ](/LTS/FAQ).

Companies using Debian who benefit from this project are encouraged to either [help directly](https://lts-team.pages.debian.net/wiki/Development.html) or [contribute financially](/LTS/Funding). The number of properly supported packages depends directly on the level of support that the LTS team receives.

All Debian LTS security advisories (DLAs) are published at <https://www.debian.org/lts/security/> where you can also subscribe via RSS feed.

Extended Long Term Support is also available. Please refer to [Extended LTS](/LTS/Extended) for further information.
