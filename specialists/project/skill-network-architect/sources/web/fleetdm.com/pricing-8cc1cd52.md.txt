## Frequently asked questions

You already know how to manage the operating system. In Fleet, you can focus on managing {{ bowser.windows ? 'Windows, Apple' : 'Apple, Windows'}} and Linux; not "Android MDM", "Apple MDM", "config management", etc.

Fleet has run over 50 workshops globally with a certification program that trains admins to be effective using gitops/infrastructure as code (with zero previous experience) in only a few hours. Check out [upcoming workshops](/gitops-workshop) in a city near you, or [let us know](/contact) where we should go next!

Fleet [started as an osquery management server](https://fleetdm.com/handbook/company#history). After 10 years of production osquery use at scale in huge organizations, Fleet added "write" and "execute" device management capabilities, with all the necessary integrations to be comparable with other device management platforms like Jamf, Omnissa (fka WS1, fka Airwatch), Microsoft Intune, and more. Today, Fleet is used by [customers in 90 countries](/customers) and in homelabs all over the world.
