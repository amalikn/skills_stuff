## Getting started

From a new account to your agent working on its first task.

1.  2:29
	Getting started · 1
	### [Start a new project with Pad](https://www.getpad.dev/learn/new-project)
	Create a Pad Cloud account, make a workspace for your project, and link it to the code on your machine, so your coding agent has a project to work from.
2.  2:58
	Getting started · 2
	### [Set up your workspace with /pad onboard](https://www.getpad.dev/learn/onboard)
	Your coding agent sets up your Pad workspace with you: it asks how you work, then shapes collections, rules and playbooks to match.
3.  2:06
	Getting started · 3
	### [Work through your first task](https://www.getpad.dev/learn/first-task)
	Ask your agent what to work on next, and follow one task from open to done, with the task in Pad kept up to date as it goes.
4.  2:14
	Getting started · 4
	### [Plan a feature with /pad plan](https://www.getpad.dev/learn/plan)
	Plan a feature with your agent: agree the goal, the scope and a task list, and end up with a plan in Pad and its tasks on your board.

## Existing projects

Bring Pad into a codebase you already have.

1.  3:55
	Existing projects · 1
	### [Add Pad to an existing project](https://www.getpad.dev/learn/existing-project)
	Already have a repository? Link it to a Pad Cloud workspace, then let your agent read the project, move your open work into Pad, and propose rules from your Makefile and commit history.
