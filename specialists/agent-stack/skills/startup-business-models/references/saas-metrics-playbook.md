# SaaS Metrics Playbook

Use consistent cohort/segment definitions and disclose calculation windows.

- MRR/ARR: recurring contracted/subscription revenue only; define treatment of usage and services.
- Logo churn: lost customers / opening customers for the period.
- Gross revenue retention: opening recurring revenue retained before expansion.
- Net revenue retention: opening recurring revenue retained after expansion.
- CAC payback: acquisition cost / monthly contribution margin from acquired cohort.
- Burn multiple: net cash burn / net new ARR, with period and accounting treatment explicit.

Avoid blended averages that hide segment differences. Pair acquisition metrics with retention and gross margin; growth without durable contribution margin can destroy value.
