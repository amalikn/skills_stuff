# Decision memo template

## Decision requested

State the exact approval, maximum capital at risk, owner, and expiry date.

## Opportunity and customer

Describe the customer problem, offer, channel, and why the pilot exists.

## Verified facts

List each actionable claim with its source key and retrieval date.

## Assumptions and estimates

List user-stated facts, proposed claims, estimates, methods, ranges, and exclusions separately from verified facts.

## Deterministic calculations

Reference calculator version, input hash, landed cost, unit contribution, cash trough, required capital, and scenario outputs.

## Risks and conditions precedent

State unresolved critical claims, owner, required evidence, mitigation, and stop/exit condition.

## Recommendation and gate

State `BLOCKED`, `READY_FOR_REVIEW`, `APPROVED`, or `DECLINED` exactly as returned by the gate. Include the approval record when applicable.
