# Source-quality policy

Use a primary source before asserting a load-bearing external fact. This covers customs classifications, duties, GST, import eligibility, biosecurity, certification, registration, product safety, consumer obligations, financial rates, market data, and third-party API behaviour.

For a regulated Australian/JDM decision, the record must identify the authoritative publisher, current/in-force version, retrieval date, operative wording, and any product or fact assumptions that affect applicability. A search-result snippet, a forum post, a reseller page, or an LLM answer does not satisfy this requirement.

When verification cannot be completed, write `UNVERIFIED` beside the claim and describe exactly what authority or document would settle it. The generic-import and Australia/JDM packs intentionally ask questions rather than embedding jurisdictional answers for this reason.

For commercial estimates, record the method, data window, currency, quantity, exclusions, and sensitivity range. Do not present estimates as published facts.
