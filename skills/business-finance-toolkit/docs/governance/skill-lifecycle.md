# Skill lifecycle

| Status | Meaning | Promotion requirement |
|---|---|---|
| `PROPOSED` | Candidate capability not yet researched. | Clear scope and owner. |
| `RESEARCHED` | Workflow and authoritative-source needs understood. | Testable output contract. |
| `PROTOTYPE` | Usable under close review. | Fixtures, validation, and known limits. |
| `VALIDATED` | Deterministic or workflow behaviour tested for declared scope. | Governance and change owner. |
| `GOVERNED` | Versioning, sources, approvals, and operational controls in place. | Operational acceptance. |
| `PRODUCTION` | Approved for its documented scope. | Periodic review evidence. |
| `DEPRECATED` | No new work; retained for traceability. | Replacement/migration note. |

Skills do not inherit `PRODUCTION` status from the toolkit. Each skill must demonstrate its own sources, tests, limits, approver, and change history. Domain packs that contain jurisdictional question sets are typically `PROTOTYPE` until a jurisdiction-specific review process exists.
