# Roadmap

## Release 0.1 — foundation

Complete: typed claim and decision model, deterministic commercial calculations, planning, gates, run manifests, generic-import and Australia/JDM question packs, portable skills, fixtures, and CLI.

## Release 0.2 — durable local operations

Add a SQLite repository with migrations, claim/source version history, signed approvals, memo rendering, and import/export. Build a DuckDB/pandas adapter for CSVs such as sales, listings, competitor observations, and freight quotes.

## Release 0.3 — guided research and pilot tracking

Add source capture templates, research queues, pilot KPI snapshots, sales/listing evaluation, operational risk registers, and decision-memo templates. Keep external research read-only until reviewed.

## Release 0.4 — governed provider integrations

Ship opt-in LLM providers through the existing contracts, prompt/data minimisation controls, provider audit records, and human review queues. Add no autonomous purchasing, publishing, filing, or communication.

## Later portfolio work

Evaluate `invoice-finance-analyst` independently against the claim, provenance, calculation, testing, lifecycle, and approval contracts. Integrate it only as a versioned specialist skill after a compatibility and remediation review.
