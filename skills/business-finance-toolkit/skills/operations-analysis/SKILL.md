# Operations Analysis

Map every hand-off from sourcing to customer outcome. Record owner, lead time, dependency, cost, quality check, fallback, and failure consequence. Highlight single points of failure and capacity constraints. Link operational risks to cash, customer promises, and pilot stop conditions.
