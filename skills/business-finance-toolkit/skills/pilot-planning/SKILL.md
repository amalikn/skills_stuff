# Pilot Planning

Choose the smallest pilot that tests the riskiest assumptions. Define quantity, duration, spend limit, owner, approval, evidence to collect, success metrics, stop-loss triggers, and exit path. A pilot begins only after critical claims are actionable and a named human approves it. Do not convert an exploratory plan into a purchase instruction.
