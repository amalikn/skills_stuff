# Business Pulse

Compare current facts with prior period, plan, and thresholds. Lead with cash runway, sales velocity, inventory exposure, operational exceptions, and unresolved risks. Link each number to a source, calculation run, or assumption. End with ranked decisions needed; do not make external changes automatically.
