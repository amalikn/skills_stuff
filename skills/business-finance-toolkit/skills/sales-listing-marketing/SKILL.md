# Sales, Listing, and Marketing Analysis

Use verified product facts only. Select channels based on customer reach, fees, lead quality, rules, and fulfilment capability. Draft a listing brief with approved facts, required disclosures, price rationale, image needs, and prohibited claims. Measure impressions, enquiries, qualified leads, conversion, gross contribution, return rate, and listing age. Publishing always needs human approval.
