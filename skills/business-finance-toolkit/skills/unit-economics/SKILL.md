# Unit Economics

Use deterministic calculator inputs. Separate selling price, variable acquisition/fulfilment cost, and selling cost. Calculate break-even units only from a positive unit contribution and a separately stated fixed-cost base. Show exclusions, tax treatment, and quantity basis. Test price, cost, sell-through, and exchange-rate sensitivities separately. Do not use gross margin as cash availability without the cash-flow and capital analysis.
