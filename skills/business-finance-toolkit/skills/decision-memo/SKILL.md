# Decision Memo

State the decision requested and the maximum commitment. Separate verified facts, user-stated context, unverified assumptions, and deterministic results. Present base and downside cases, missing critical evidence, key risks, conditions precedent, and the gate result. Use `BLOCKED` or `READY_FOR_REVIEW` exactly as returned; do not write `APPROVED` without a named human approver.
