# Opportunity Assessment

Define the customer, painful job, alternative solutions, offer, channel, expected price, cost drivers, dependencies, and disconfirming evidence. Convert unknowns into named claims. Separate user assumptions from observed evidence. Do not issue a proceed recommendation until market, economics, and risk questions have a defined evidence path.
