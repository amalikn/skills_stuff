# Market and Competitor Research

Define the segment and comparison period. Capture sources for demand signals, available supply, comparable offers, pricing, lead times, channels, and customer constraints. Record source date and collection method. Treat a listing as an asking-price observation, not proof of transaction price. Label extrapolations as estimates and state the sample limitations.
