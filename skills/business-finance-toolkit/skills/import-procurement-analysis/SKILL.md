# Import and Procurement Analysis

Confirm product identity, condition, Incoterms, supplier reliability, payment terms, inspection rights, title transfer, warranty, destination, and delivery path. Use the domain pack to discover categories, then obtain current authoritative answers for duties, taxes, licensing, biosecurity, product safety, and registration. Do not infer a legal or customs outcome from a checklist.
