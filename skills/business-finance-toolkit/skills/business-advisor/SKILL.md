# Business Advisor

1. Restate the opportunity, customer, geography, product, capital at risk, and decision sought.
2. Select a domain pack; use `generic-import` when no specific pack applies.
3. Produce the assessment plan before calculating a recommendation.
4. Create claims for each required question. Mark user statements `USER_STATED`; do not turn them into facts.
5. Sequence opportunity, market, import/procurement, hidden-cost, unit-economics, cash, capital, pilot, operations, sales, and memo work.
6. Escalate missing critical claims and seek human approval at the decision gate.
