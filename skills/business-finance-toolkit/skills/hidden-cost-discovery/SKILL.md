# Hidden-Cost Discovery

Start with the transaction and draft cost model. Compare it with the relevant pack's categories. For every category, mark `included`, `not-applicable` with reason, `estimated` with method, or `missing`. Cover origin fees, transport, inspection, documents, storage, freight, insurance, border handling, domestic logistics, compliance, preparation, FX, finance, sales, warranty, returns, holding cost, and contingency.
