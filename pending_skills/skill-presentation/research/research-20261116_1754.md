# Research record 3: a short meeting deck for a first-time senior audience

Source: one working session in apn enterprise-strategy, 16 Nov 2026, preparing a 60-minute update with the sponsor (Martin) and his manager (Sandro, seeing the program for the first time).

| #   | Lesson                                                                                                                                                          | Label                                                   |
| --- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | A senior reader seeing the work for the first time gets a short deck built from existing slides, not the full pack or deck: seven slides, about 22 minutes, half the slot left for questions. | USER_STATED (operator, 16 Nov 2026)                     |
| 2   | Time per slide is a ceiling, not a target: no more than three to four minutes a slide, key bullet points only; "less is more".                                  | USER_STATED (sponsor via operator, Teams, 16 Nov 2026)  |
| 3   | Detail diagrams (Gantt chart, timeline fishbones, Ishikawa) go after the decisions slide as backup, behind a divider, opened only when a question needs them.    | USER_STATED (operator, 16 Nov 2026)                     |
| 4   | The question the sponsor expects ("do we still need this now that the vendor is supported?") is answered on the second content slide, before anyone asks it; the speaker notes carry the definition of any term the answer depends on. | USER_STATED (sponsor's warning; operator's placement)   |
| 5   | The last main slide lists the decisions needed, each with what it unblocks; a recommendation not to do something (not moving back to the vendor's cloud) is its own decision so it is agreed explicitly. | USER_STATED (operator, 16 Nov 2026)                     |
| 6   | Use the business's product terms on slides (here "SMC" for the site box; a vendor name only where the design already names it); keep vendor-neutral wording for a role, or the deck contradicts its own argument against single-vendor dependence. | USER_STATED (operator) and a side review, 16 Nov 2026   |
| 7   | pptxgenjs 3.12.0 writes Company "PptxGenJS" and Subject "PptxGenJS Presentation" into every deck unless set; set both when the presentation is created and check `docProps/` before a deck leaves the team. | VERIFIED_PRIMARY (pptxgenjs `dist/pptxgen.cjs.js`)      |

Also seen: send the short deck to the sponsor for review the evening before, with a three-paragraph cover message (what it is, the hard question's answer, the ask).

Build mechanics (reused builders, a wording table, a byte-identical proof) are recorded in skill-coder `references/design-patterns.md` P-72.
