# Research for skill-presentation

Keep every piece of research here, beside the seed, and never delete or move it after the pack is built: the seed's `Source material` and `What was learned` tables point at these files, and a later revision of the
pack starts here. One file per research pass, named `research-YYYYMMDD_hhmm.md`, with each claim labelled `VERIFIED_PRIMARY`, `VERIFIED_SECONDARY`, `USER_STATED` or `UNVERIFIED` and its source. Verbatim captures of
external pages go in a subfolder with a provenance header and a sha256 row here. The build copies what the finished pack needs into the pack's own `documents/`; this folder stays as the record.
